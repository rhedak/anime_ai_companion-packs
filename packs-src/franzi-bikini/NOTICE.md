# Franzi (bikini) picture pack

An original character: **Franzi**, in a white halter bikini, with her black hair in a low ponytail (no bow). No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The neutral base picture was made with an AI image tool (xAI's Grok Imagine) from Franzi's existing full-body bikini reference picture (from the `anime-character-creator` repository), cropped to the same head-to-mid-thigh framing as the casual-outfit base picture, with her hair in a low ponytail and no bow. The six emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`,
`shy`, `thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made by editing that neutral base picture in the same tool and are used as they came, scaled to 940 × 1070 (the tool zoomed out on `cheer-2`, so her figure is smaller there and her raised hands touch the top edge). The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three
mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven emotions were **not** made by an image tool: a script drew
closed eyes and an open mouth over the face of each emotion picture, and left every other pixel identical. They
are simpler than the hand-finished pictures of the other packs.

The background of every picture is transparent. It was removed with a script from the plain black background the pictures were made on: black pixels connected to the picture's edge, and the black gaps between her arms and body, between her fingers, and between her hair, neck and shoulders, were made transparent.

Whether pictures made by an image model can be copyrighted, and by whom, is not settled everywhere, and the

of the rest of the repository, which grants whatever rights the repository's owner has in them and cannot grant more
than exists. This is not legal advice. `docs/frame-packs.md` says how to make your own pack.
