# Risa (school uniform) picture pack

An original character: **Risa**, in her school uniform (a buttoned navy blazer with gold buttons, a white shirt with a light blue ribbon bow and a blue-grey pleated skirt), with long straight dark red hair with bangs, green eyes and a gold hair clip. No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** Risa's full-body school uniform reference picture was made with an AI image tool (xAI's Grok Imagine) from her bikini reference picture (`ref/risa-adult` in the `anime-character-creator` repository). The seven emotions (`neutral`, `happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`, `shy`, `thinking`) and the four poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) were made by editing that full-body uniform picture in the same tool. Each picture was then cropped to the same head-to-mid-thigh framing as the `risa-bikini` pack and enlarged about 1.27 times, so the pictures are a little softer than the bikini ones; the `cheer-2` crop was moved up so her raised hands stay in the frame, so her face sits slightly lower there. They were scaled to 940 × 1070. The blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three
mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of the seven emotions were **not** made by an image tool: a script drew
closed eyes and an open mouth over the face of each emotion picture, and left every other pixel identical. Her eyes are already closed in `happy`, so `happy-blink` is the same picture as `happy`. They
are simpler than the hand-finished pictures of the other packs.

The background of every picture is transparent. It was removed with a script from the plain white background the pictures were made on: white pixels connected to the picture's edge, and the white gaps between her arms and body, between her fingers, between her hair and her arms, and between the strands of her hair, were made transparent. Her white shirt, collar and cuffs and the whites of her eyes are kept.
