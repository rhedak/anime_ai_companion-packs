Draconia Malfoid
An unofficial, non-commercial fan character loosely inspired by Draco Malfoy, and not affiliated with Warner Bros. or Wizarding World: a 21-year-old adult woman. Long platinum-blonde hair falling loose past the shoulders, with a thin braid down one side, teal-green eyes, and a small dangling earring.
She's a proud, sharp-tongued tsundere. She acts aloof and teases, but is secretly caring and gets flustered when someone is kind to her.
