# Risa (green coat) picture pack

An original character: **Risa**, in a winter outfit (a dark green hooded coat over a white knitted sweater and a grey wool skirt), with long straight dark red hair with bangs, green eyes and a gold hair clip. No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI**, with xAI's Grok Imagine. The neutral picture is an edit of the neutral picture of an unpublished draft outfit of Risa (hot pants and a short top, which was itself made from the `risa-dress` pack with the image model FLUX.2 [klein] 4B on the owner's computer): the prompt changed her outfit and left the rest alone. The six other emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`, `shy`, `thinking`) were made by editing that neutral picture, and so were the blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of every emotion. For these only a box around her face was kept from the model's result; everything else is the neutral picture unchanged. The poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) and `thinking` (a hand on her chin) are whole-picture edits, because an arm leaves the face box.

The pictures were made by the pack studio of the anime_ai_companion app (`docs/pack-studio.md`), which drew each one on the Grok Imagine website, in the owner's own signed-in Chrome, through Claude in Chrome. The background of every picture is transparent: they were made on flat magenta, which the model cannot make transparent, and the studio cut her out with soft edges (known-background matting), with the background colour taken out of the edge pixels.
