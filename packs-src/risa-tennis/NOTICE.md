# Risa (tennis) picture pack

An original character: **Risa**, in a tennis outfit (a white short-sleeved polo shirt with navy trim and a short white pleated skirt), with long straight dark red hair with bangs, green eyes and a gold hair clip, with a tennis activity. No real person is shown.

## How it was made

**These pictures were made with AI.** The neutral picture is an edit of the neutral picture of the `risa-bikini` pack (itself made with xAI's Grok Imagine), made with ChatGPT (OpenAI's image model, on a free account): the prompt changed her outfit and left the rest alone. Every other still was made with xAI's Grok Imagine: the six other emotions (`happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `surprised`, `shy`, `thinking`) by editing the neutral picture, and so were the blink (`<emotion>-blink`) and the three mouth openings (`<emotion>-talk1` to `-talk3`) of every emotion. For these only a box around her face was kept from the model's result. The poses (`wave-1`, `wave-2`, `cheer-1`, `cheer-2`) and `thinking` (a hand on her chin) are whole-picture edits.

The tennis activity (`activity-tennis-1` to `-70`, a 5.8-second loop at 12 pictures a second): a picture of her holding a racket was made with Grok Imagine from the neutral picture on a flat magenta background, and animated with Grok Imagine (video, 720p, 6 seconds): a ball flies in from the lower left, she swings and hits it, and it flies toward the viewer and out of the picture; she returns to the start pose. Frames of the video were cut out one by one.

The pictures were made by the pack studio of the anime_ai_companion app (`docs/pack-studio.md`), which drew them on the ChatGPT and Grok Imagine websites in the owner's own signed-in Chrome through Claude in Chrome. The background of every picture is transparent: they were made on flat magenta, which the models cannot make transparent, and a script cut her out with soft edges (known-background matting).
